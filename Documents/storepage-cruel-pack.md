# MURMUR Cruel Pack

## Short Summary
Unfiltered mob hostility. Mobs insult your gear, mock your health, trash-talk your choices, and refuse to let you feel good about anything.

---

## Description

**MURMUR Cruel Pack** is a content pack for MURMUR Core that replaces benign ambient chatter with something rawer: mobs that actively disrespect you.

Villagers whisper behind your back. Pillagers catalogue your gear with contempt. Piglins question your relevance. This pack takes the living-world immersion of MURMUR and weaponizes it.

**Highlights:**
- Villagers roast your held item and comment on your health bar with zero sympathy
- Pillagers and Vindicators treat you as a tactical problem to be solved, not a hero
- Piglins are obsessed with gold and deeply unimpressed by your presence
- Witches are clinical, unsettling, and mildly amused by your mortality
- Full situational awareness — night, rain, thunder, raid, boat, wounds, fire, nether
- Supports `{item}` and `{health}` placeholders for hyper-specific insults based on what you're holding and how injured you are

> *"Oh great, the barely holding together loser is carrying a pathetic splinter. Peak comedy."*

---

## ⚠️ Warnings & Clarifications

- **Requires MURMUR Core** — will not function without it. Install Core first, then this pack.
- **Enable Beta APIs** in world settings.
- Dialogue is intentionally irreverent and meta (mobs may reference game mechanics). If you want immersive/serious tone, use the Medieval Pack instead.
- One content pack at a time is recommended alongside Core.
- **Requires Minecraft Bedrock 1.21.0+**
