🕒 Option 1: Time of Day Reactions (Night Terror)
What it adds: Mobs react dynamically to the moon rising or midnight. Villagers panic about monsters; hostiles get more confident.

Why it's easy: Like weather, the time of day is a global property already tracked by the engine (world.getTimeOfDay()). You don’t need to look up any complex entity components or run heavy structural checks.

Implementation: You just check the current tick time inside your main interval loop. If it's between dusk and dawn (e.g., tick 13000 to 23000), you swap their speech array over to a "night" pool.

Dialogue Vibe: * Villagers: "The dark time is here. The things that click are coming." or "Jeff, did you lock the door? JEFF!"

Pillagers: "The light is gone. Move out."

🩹 Option 2: Health Status Triggers (Wounded Dialogue)
What it adds: When a mob gets low on health, their ambient proximity dialogue shifts to desperate, exhausted, or furious variations.

Why it's easy: Every living entity has a built-in health component (EntityHealthComponent). Your loop can check their current health against their max health in two lines of code without using heavy event listeners.

Implementation: ```javascript
const healthComp = entity.getComponent("minecraft:health");
if (healthComp && healthComp.currentValue <= healthComp.defaultValue / 3) {
// Use low-health dialogue pool instead!
}

Witches: "My blood... it's ruining the mixture..."

Villagers: "Everything hurts... I want to go home."

🏃 Option 3: Basic Movement State Checks (Sprinting vs. Idle)
What it adds: Mobs say different things depending on whether they are completely still or aggressively running from something.

Why it's easy: You don't have to calculate complex velocity math. The engine already attaches simple velocity vectors (entity.velocity) or basic speed components directly to them. If their velocity on the X or Z axis is high, they are running.

Implementation: A quick check inside the interval loop to see if the absolute value of their horizontal velocity is above a tiny threshold. If it is, they use a "moving" or "running" dialogue pool.

Dialogue Vibe:

Villagers (Running): "TOO FAST! OUT OF THE WAY!"

Pillagers (Moving): "Double time! Don't let them lose us!"
