# MURMUR Uncanny Pack

## Short Summary
Liminal, philosophical, slightly broken mob chatter. Mobs talk like they half-remember they're in a game — but can't quite put it into words.

---

## Description

**MURMUR Uncanny Pack** is a content pack for MURMUR Core that replaces mob ambient dialogue with something unsettling, introspective, and strange.

These mobs aren't quite right. Villagers forget what they were doing mid-sentence. They describe blocks as "the clicky things" and refer to the sun as "the yellow sky-beast." Piglins talk about gold like it's a religion. Pillagers speak with quiet, systematic menace. The Evoker sounds like a mathematician who's gone off the deep end.

This is MURMUR for players who want their world to feel *liminal* — like something is slightly off, always.

**Highlights:**
- Villagers mutter about things that can't quite be explained — dreams about stone boxes, sheep that stopped talking, eyes in the sky
- Mobs describe the world in fragmented, semi-aware terms (`"The orange block is spreading!"`, `"I'm leaking... why am I leaking?!"`)
- Pillagers treat the raid like a systems operation and casualties like a data loss event
- Piglins and Brutes have fully distinct internal cultures — bartering obsession vs pure violence
- Contextual placeholder support — mobs notice your item and health in an off, eerie way
  > *"Why is he carrying a {item}? It looks like it doesn't belong in this world."*
  > *"You are {health}... like a block that's about to be broken."*
- 1,100+ unique lines covering villagers, pillagers, vindicators, evokers, piglins, brutes, and witches

---

## ⚠️ Warnings & Clarifications

- **Requires MURMUR Core** — will not function without it. Install Core first, then this pack.
- **Enable Beta APIs** in world settings.
- This pack is intentionally weird. Villagers will say things that break the fourth wall slightly. This is the design.
- The Uncanny Pack has the largest dialogue database (~1,100 lines). Performance impact remains minimal due to chunked loading.
- One content pack at a time is recommended alongside Core.
- **Requires Minecraft Bedrock 1.21.0+**
