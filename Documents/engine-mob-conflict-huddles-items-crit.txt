🛠️ Code Implementation: How to execute it cleanly
To add the Environmental Check or Inter-Mob Conflict without slowing down your entityIterator loop, you just add one or two quick conditional wrappers inside the entity loop in main.js right before picking the dialogue line:

JavaScript
// Example: Deep Underground check inside your main iterator loop
let state = "idle";
if (entity.location.y < 0) {
    state = "deep_dark";
} else if (entity.getComponent("minecraft:onfire")) {
    state = "on_fire";
}

That is a very sharp gut instinct. Giving Zombies standard dialogue lines can instantly break the entire survival illusion. If a regular, rotting corpse starts speaking coherent, full sentences, it doesn't feel scary or vanilla anymore—it feels like a cartoon or a bad comedy.

There are two completely different ways to handle this in your documentation, depending on what feels right for the ScalarLabs style:

### Option A: The "Gutteral Mind" Approach (Pure Atmosphere)

Instead of speech, their chat messages are internal monologue fragments, single words, or involuntary motor responses slipping past their broken vocal cords. In `utils.js`, you can give them a unique color style like `§2` (Dark Green) or `§8` (Dark Gray) to show their text is different from human speech.

* **Idle / Wandering:**
* `"*teeth grinding together*"`
* `"Cold... so cold..."`
* `"...more..."`
* `"*heavy breathing*"`


* **Chasing a Player or Villager:**
* `"Warm... inside..."`
* `"Stop... running..."`
* `"Give it... back..."`
* `"Smell... them..."`


* **Burning in daylight (Dawn):**
* `"THE YELLOW SIGHT BURNSSSS!"`
* `"MELTING..."`
* `"*flesh blistering*"`



### Option B: The "Silent Void" Approach (Zero Chatter)

You can completely blacklist zombies from chatting normally. Instead, they **only exist as triggers for other mobs**. This preserves their brainless nature while making the world around them much more reactive.

* **Villager spotting a Zombie:** * `"The dead walk! Lock the doors!"`
* `"Oh no... that used to be old {name}..."`


* **Iron Golem targeting a Zombie:** * `"THREAT RECOGNIZED. PURGING."`
* `"BACK TO THE SOIL."`


* **Skeleton witnessing a Zombie get crushed:** * `"Useless pile of meat."`
* `"He didn't even have any good bones left anyway."`



If you want them completely silent, you just don't add `"minecraft:zombie"` to `DIALOGUE` in `dialogue.js`. If you want the eerie, broken-speech style, Option A keeps it grounded without losing the horror element.
