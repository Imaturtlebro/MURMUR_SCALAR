MYTHICAL / ARCHAIC: High-fantasy, Shakespearean, or Tolkienesque dialogue. Mobs speak with grand elegance, using words like "Thou," "Thee," and "Vile trespasser." It elevates a standard fantasy or medieval world overhaul. Does thou even hoist (from chivalry 2, a funnny voice line. was thiking could do that for the mythical archaic or medieval stuff. a villager coudl say it)

If you put that in a Medieval, Mythical Archaic, or even a dedicated Chivalric / Gladiator content pack, it would match perfectly. Picture an Iron Golem looking at a zombie it just tossed 20 blocks into the air and dropping that exact text line.

Since your engine uses tokens like {item} and {health}, you can take inspiration directly from those incredible Chivalry 2 voice lines and adapt them into top-tier medieval dialogue tables. Here is a quick layout of how you could map those exact vibes into your framework:

🏰 The "Chivalric" Dialogue Table (Inspired by Chiv 2)
🏋️ Proximity (Idle Chat)
"Dost thou even hoist, good sir?" (Perfect for an Iron Golem or a high-tier Armorer Villager)

"Thou art endowed with an enormous gift... many men must look up to thee!"

"I try to sound intimidating!" (Amazing for a tiny baby villager or a weak mob)

⚔️ Threat Detected (When an attack starts)
"Your stupidity is no accident, it is your birthright!" (When a zombie steps up)

"Vacate my premises, thou smellest of horse piss!" (Villager detecting a raid)

"Now I should warn you, I've been known to halve melons in the practice yard!"

"Your wife is a hobbyhorse!"

🩹 Peer Hurt / Low Health (Using your {health} or {hurt_name} tokens)
"My mother is NOT a hobbyhorse!" (Reaction when a nearby squad member gets insulted or hit)

"HERE, DEFEND ME! DEFEND MEEE!" (Squire boy style for a low-health villager running away)

"Thou standest rock hard, like a tower!" (Saying this to a nearby Iron Golem when a raid hits)

💀 Peer Death / Defeat (When a squad member dies)
"See you in hell!" (Delivered in the most completely cheery voice possible right as they swing)

"Wallow in the muck, you disgusting pig!"

Why this kills it for your modular setup
Gamers love Easter eggs. Because you've already built the entire MURMUR Core backend to do the heavy processing, you don't have to code a single new mechanic to make this happen. You can just sit down, type out a custom JSON table of pure, unadulterated Chivalry roasts, and drop it as its own add-on pack.
