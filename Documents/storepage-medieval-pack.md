# MURMUR Medieval Pack

## Short Summary
High-stakes medieval character voice. Mobs speak with the weight of duty, history, and impending dread — like they know they're living inside a story.

---

## Description

**MURMUR Medieval Pack** is a content pack for MURMUR Core that transforms mob chatter into immersive, period-authentic medieval dialogue. Every mob sounds like they've lived through something.

Villagers speak of omens, soil, and harvest. They warn by candlelight and mourn by torchlight. Skeletons clatter with ominous purpose. Golems stand in silent sentinel vigil. Illagers march with the certainty of conquerors.

**Highlights:**
- Archaic vocabulary and cadence — "Prithee", "Verily", "By the high king"
- Villagers comment on weather, night creatures, their lord's decrees, and the weight of the world
- Situational depth — thunder storms, midnight raids, fire, wounds, drowning in mud
- Mobs react to your held item and health in a thematically consistent way
  > *"Tell me, friend... why dost thou brandish that pathetic splinter in our village?"*
  > *"Thou art one foot in the grave... a grim sight indeed for an adventurer."*
- Full emotional range: quiet dread, dark humor, desperate courage

---

## ⚠️ Warnings & Clarifications

- **Requires MURMUR Core** — will not function without it. Install Core first, then this pack.
- **Enable Beta APIs** in world settings.
- This pack is designed for vanilla Minecraft worlds with a medieval/fantasy feel. It coexists with most aesthetic mods.
- One content pack at a time is recommended alongside Core.
- **Requires Minecraft Bedrock 1.21.0+**
