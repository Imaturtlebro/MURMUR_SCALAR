In Fallout: New Vegas, Malcolm Holmes is the guy who tracks you down the exact second you pick up your very first Sunset Sarsaparilla Blue Star Bottle Cap.

No matter where you are in the desert—sleeping in a bed, sneaking past Deathclaws, or in the middle of an intense firefight—Malcolm silently spawns, sprints across the entire map, and forces you into a locked dialogue camera to tell you about the "legend of the Star Caps." Because he jumpscares players in the worst possible moments, almost every Fallout player kills him on sight (especially since he carries six Star Caps on his corpse).

Building a "Malcolm Holmes" mechanic in Bedrock with your MURMUR engine would be hilarious and absurdly effective.

How to Hook It Up
The Item Trigger:
Listen for the player picking up a specific "trigger" item in script (e.g., a minecraft:echo_shard, a rare "Blue Emerald", or a custom item).

Out-of-Sight Spawn:
When triggered, calculate a position 15–20 blocks behind the player’s view vector (where they aren't looking) and spawn a custom NPC entity (murmur:persistent_stranger).

The Relentless Sprint:
Set the entity's movement speed high and pathfind directly to player.location.

Forced Dialogue Lockout:
As soon as distanceTo(player) < 2.5, clear the entity's target, fire your UI form trigger in script, and force-open the MURMUR dialogue menu on the player's screen.
